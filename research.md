# Research

My research lies at the intersection of **category theory, quantum theory, mathematics, and theoretical computer science**.

I am particularly interested in categorical structures that provide mathematical frameworks for describing quantum systems, and in extending categorical quantum mechanics to settings involving infinite-dimensional systems.

## PhD Research

### Infinite-Dimensional Categorical Quantum Mechanics

My doctoral research investigates mathematical structures for developing **infinite-dimensional categorical quantum mechanics**.

The project explores how categorical models of quantum theory can be extended beyond the finite-dimensional setting, with particular attention to the interaction between categorical structures, operator theory, and quantum dynamics.

Current topics include:

- Non-compact dagger-linear and *-autonomous categories
- Linear Distributive Categories
- Mixed Unitary Categories
- Operator spaces and operator algebras
- Differential categorical structures
- Infinite-dimensional quantum harmonic oscillators
- Continuous observables and projection-valued measures

The project is being developed jointly at **Université Paris-Saclay** and **Macquarie University**.

## Research interests

### Category Theory

Categorical structures and their applications to mathematics and theoretical computer science, particularly as a language for describing mathematical theories and their relationships.

### Categorical Quantum Mechanics

Diagrammatic and categorical approaches to quantum theory, including dagger categories, compact structures, Frobenius algebras, and categorical models of quantum processes.

### Operator Theory

Operator spaces, operator algebras, completely bounded maps, and their connections with categorical and quantum structures.

### Mathematical Physics

Mathematical structures underlying quantum theories, including infinite-dimensional systems and quantum dynamics.
